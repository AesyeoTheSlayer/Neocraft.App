Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit NeoCraft or subscribe to project updates.

Effective August 19, 2026

Information you provide

If you subscribe to NeoCraft updates, you provide an email address. The subscription form sends that address directly to Buttondown, the service used to manage the newsletter, confirmation process, delivery, and unsubscribing. NeoCraft does not operate a separate subscriber database on this website.

Information processed automatically

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub and the network providers involved in delivering the site may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser or device information, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs. This processing is controlled by those providers and is used to deliver, protect, and maintain their services.

How information is used

  • To deliver requested NeoCraft development updates
  • To confirm and manage newsletter subscriptions
  • To create accounts and verify email addresses for community features
  • To operate community discussions, moderation, and issue tracking
  • To operate, secure, and troubleshoot the website
  • To respond to project or privacy-related questions

Alpha testing and bug reports

If you submit a test report, NeoCraft receives the information you choose to include. This may include your name or username, email address, browser and operating-system details, device information, reproduction steps, screenshots, logs, or other information needed to understand the report. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, session tokens, or unrelated personal information.

Forums and issue tracking

If you participate in the community forums, your display name, discussion titles, posts, replies, and posting dates are public. Your account email address is not displayed with forum posts. Do not publish personal information, game files, passwords, authentication codes, or access tokens.

Issue reports may contain your account identifier, contact email, report type and severity, build information, browser, operating system, device details, reproduction steps, expected and actual results, and evidence links you choose to provide. Reports stored in the issue tracker are visible only to the submitting account and authorized NeoCraft moderators or administrators.

Unfinished forum discussions and issue reports may be saved locally in your browser as drafts. These drafts remain on your device until submitted, cleared from the form, or removed with the site’s browser storage.

NeoCraft accounts

If you create a NeoCraft account, the account service processes your email address, chosen display name, securely hashed password credentials, email-confirmation state, and account identifier. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider and are not visible to NeoCraft.

NeoCraft uses Supabase to provide account authentication and database access controls for community features. Account sessions are stored in the browser so you can remain signed in. The public test client does not require a NeoCraft account.

World data and browser storage

The test client may store worlds, settings, and related game data locally through browser storage. Local game data remains on the tester’s device unless the tester deliberately exports or sends it. Clearing browser data, changing builds, or browser failures may remove or invalidate locally stored test data.

When a tester selects a compatible Minecraft client JAR or optional audio resources, the test client reads and verifies those files locally in the browser. NeoCraft does not upload or permanently store the selected game files, and they may need to be selected again after the page is reloaded.

Sharing and service providers

NeoCraft does not sell account, report, or subscriber information. Information is processed by services necessary to operate the website, accounts, account-email delivery, issue reports, and newsletter, principally GitHub Pages, Formspree, Supabase, Resend, and Buttondown. Their own privacy policies govern information they process:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Buttondown Privacy Policy
  • Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Resend Privacy Policy

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

NeoCraft does not currently run Google AdSense or a site analytics service. If advertising or analytics is added, this policy will be updated before activation to explain the providers, cookies or similar technologies involved, available choices, and any required consent controls.

Your choices

Every newsletter email includes controls for unsubscribing or managing your subscription. You may use those controls at any time. For other privacy questions or requests, email preston@andgroup.net.

Children’s privacy

NeoCraft accounts, community features, issue reports, and the newsletter are not intended for children under 13. Anyone under 13 should not create an account, submit a report, or provide an email address.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website’s features or service providers change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when material changes are published.
